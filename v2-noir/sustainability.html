Sustainability

A trip to our Garden

At Reguentex, our sustainability initiatives extend beyond textile production and into the garden as well! We grow our own fresh, organic vegetables onsite and use them to create a delicious daily soup for our employees. We’ve worked hard to optimise the space we have available which means we can grow many different types of vegetables organically, without the use of any pesticides or chemicals.
